## The short answer.

The Monthly sales report template formats the month's sales figures: a table of gaps against targets and the previous month, then a report with highlights, gaps to explain (causes marked "hypothèse") and proposed actions. No figure comes from anywhere but your data.

A workflow template is an automated sequence of tasks, provided as a starting point in WorkStudio and to be adapted to the business before it is deployed.

## The steps.

#### Build the gap table.

An agent compares each indicator with the target and the previous month; an impossible comparison is marked "[donnée manquante]".

#### Write the report.

A second agent writes the highlights, the gaps to explain (causes as "hypothèse") and the actions, with no figure absent from the table.

#### Review.

You read the text produced, correct it if needed, then use or send it yourself: the template sends nothing.

## Your questions about this template.

#### Does this template send anything for me?

No. It produces text to review; you use or send it yourself. Only five templates in the collection send an email, and each waits first for a person's approval.

#### Does the template connect to my sales software?

No. You paste the figures in the starting field. Connecting to your software goes through a connector, qualified at the diagnostic.

#### Does it suit Quebec as is?

Not as is. The starter templates are written for a French small business and do not follow Quebec tax rules: taxes, formats and vocabulary are adapted to your business.
