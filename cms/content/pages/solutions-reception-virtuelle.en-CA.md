## One reception, three channels.

Phone calls, emails and text messages arrive in one place. The virtual reception understands the request, answers with your information (hours, services, policies) and acts: it adds the appointment to your calendar, updates your customer management tool or passes the call to the right person.

Every exchange ends with a written summary, sent to whoever you choose.

## What it does for you.

#### Answer calls.

It picks up, understands the request and answers with your information. It routes the call to the right person when needed.

#### Answer emails.

It reads incoming emails, answers common questions and sends the follow-up messages you have planned.

#### Answer text messages.

Appointment confirmation, arrival time, quick question: the answer goes out by text.

#### Book appointments.

It checks your calendar, offers open slots and books the appointment; a reminder can go out the day before.

#### Qualify and transfer.

It asks the right questions, recognizes an emergency and transfers to the right person, with the context.

#### Summarize every exchange.

A clear summary after every call, email or text: who, what, what was done and what is left to do.

## Connected to your systems.

The virtual reception connects to what you already use: your phone line, your email, your calendar and your customer management tool. Possible connections are checked during the consultation, system by system.

- **Your line.** Your number stays yours; the reception takes calls according to the rules you set: hours, overflow, night.
- **Your rules.** What it may say, what it must transfer, who to reach in an emergency: you decide, in writing.
- **Your data.** Exchanges and summaries stay at your site or on our servers, depending on the mode you choose.

## Your questions about the reception.

#### Do callers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. The reception introduces itself as your company's virtual assistant, and callers can always ask to speak to a person.

#### What happens if it does not know the answer?

It says so, takes the contact details and transfers the request to the right person, with a summary. It does not make things up: its answers come from the information you give it.

#### Does it work in French and English?

Yes. It answers in the language of the person calling or writing.

#### How much does it cost?

There is no public price yet. Two things are paid for: our services (a written estimate follows the consultation) and platform usage, in credits. Prices will appear on the Pricing page as soon as they are published.
