## Who controls what.

Eight points, from hardware to contract exit. In each case, the answer is written before go-live.

- **The hardware.** On your premises: it belongs to you. With us: it belongs to our infrastructure; you buy nothing.
- **The data.** On your premises: on your server. With us: in our infrastructure in Quebec, at the locations specified in writing.
- **The models.** In both cases: on the server concerned, or external only if you accepted them in writing. Each model shows where it runs.
- **The backups.** On your premises: wherever you put them. With us: at the locations named in the contract, with the list of subcontractors.
- **The updates.** Listed in both modes as outbound flows. On your premises, their timing is agreed; with us, we apply them.
- **Nexora's access.** On your premises: only what you grant, agreed in advance. With us: what operations require, described in the study.
- **The operations.** On your premises: shared according to the contract. With us: provided by Nexora with you.
- **Contract exit.** In both modes: your documents, configurations and workflows stay exportable.

## How to choose your mode.

There is no right mode in itself. These are the questions that steer the answer.

#### How sensitive the data is.

The more sensitive it is, the more physical control of a server on your premises is worth.

#### In-house skills.

Without a technical team, the hosted mode removes monitoring and updates from your workload.

#### Hardware and space.

On your premises, you need a server, a suitable room and network. With us, none of that.

#### The investment.

Buying hardware is an investment; hosting is a service. The cost of each option is estimated in writing after the diagnosis.

#### Your customers' requirements.

Some customers, public bodies in particular, may require hosting in Quebec: to be checked in each contract or tender.

## Deployment: your questions.

#### Which mode should I start with?

The one that matches how sensitive your data is and your in-house skills. The deployment study compares the two options for your case.

#### Can we change mode later?

Export of your documents, configurations and workflows is provided for in the contract. A change of mode is then planned as a project.

#### Does my data leave Quebec?

It depends on the mode and the models chosen; we write it down before kickoff. An external model is never configured without your written agreement.

#### Is there an offline mode?

We do not promise operation with no outbound flow at all: updates and licences are listed. Running with no outbound flow is decided and verified case by case.
