## The short answer.

The Competitor watch note template produces a structured note from extracts you paste: established facts (each quoted), weak signals marked "hypothèse", implications for you and information to look for. A fact found in no extract does not appear.

A workflow template is an automated sequence of tasks, provided as a starting point in WorkStudio and to be adapted to the business before it is deployed.

## The steps.

#### Establish the facts.

An agent lists the facts, numbered, quotes the exact passage from each extract and separates doubtful or unverifiable claims.

#### Write the note.

A second agent writes the facts, the weak signals ("hypothèse", backed by a quoted extract), the implications for you and the information to confirm.

#### Review.

You read the text produced, correct it if needed, then use or send it yourself: the template sends nothing.

## To adapt before use.

#### What to adapt here.

The template searches nothing on the web: it works only on the extracts you paste. Check each extract's source before deciding.

#### What applies to all.

The templates are written for a Quebec small business: amounts in Canadian dollars, GST and QST wherever a tax applies, and Quebec business vocabulary. They do not guarantee any tax or legal compliance: the examples are fictional, and your accountant or adviser validates the result before use.

#### Nothing is sent.

The template produces text to review. Only five templates in the collection send an email, and each waits first for a person's approval.

## Your questions about this template.

#### Does this template send anything for me?

No. It produces text to review; you use or send it yourself. Only five templates in the collection send an email, and each waits first for a person's approval.

#### Will the template look up information about the competitor?

No. It works only on the extracts you provide, and invents nothing. A web search tool exists in WorkStudio, but it is off until an administrator turns it on.

#### Is it suited to a Quebec business?

It is written for Quebec but still needs validating: the examples are fictional, and the template does not replace your accountant or adviser. Review every result before using it and adapt the instructions to your business.
