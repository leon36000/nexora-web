## Three processes, three builds.

Custom work begins where standard solutions stop. Three built examples that do not resemble each other; they are not customer results.

Does yours look like one of them, or none? [Plan a diagnosis](/en/contact/?sujet=diagnostic).

- **A contractor finds their quotes again.** They want to start from older, comparable quotes to prepare a new one, then track real costs afterward. We would connect the quote database, an estimating agent and an approval step: the estimator approves the price before anything is sent. For construction, <a href="/en/opsys-ai/">OPSYS-AI</a> aims at this path, as a product in development that shows every availability level.
- **A distributor processes its suppliers' documents.** Delivery slips, order confirmations and invoices arrive in different formats. The workflow reads them, compares them with each other and flags what does not match; the purchasing person decides before any action.
- **A firm prepares case files.** It gathers authorized documents, draws a summary and a list of points to check from them, then hands over to the responsible partner, who decides. Access rights are defined before the build.

## Six steps, from observation to operation.

#### 1. Observe.

We look at how the work gets done: where time is lost, what information comes in, who decides.

#### 2. Prototype.

We describe the workflow in plain French; the WorkStudio Copilot proposes a first version that we review and correct with you.

#### 3. Connect.

We hook up your software through connectors, after checking that each one can be connected.

#### 4. Test.

We build a test set from your real cases and measure whether the agent answers well, before changing anything.

#### 5. Approve.

Every step that commits the business waits for a person. We decide with you which ones.

#### 6. Train and operate.

Your team learns to supervise and correct; we track what works and adjust the rest.

## What we build with.

Everything is built in [Nexora WorkStudio](/en/workstudio/), a platform at the leading edge of AI where you build with cards or on a canvas, without writing code. That keeps the system readable and portable. Its components are delivered; it is their assembly for your case that is custom. Your data stays yours and is never used to train a model: [see the commitments](/en/your-data/).

- **Agents.** Assistants that follow your instructions, use your tools and your documents, and cite their sources.
- **Workflows.** Sequences of steps, triggered by an email, a schedule or a request, with human approvals.
- **Documents and data.** Knowledge bases, reading of scanned documents, tables, batch processing.
- **Connectors and an API.** Services linked through Nexora Connect, and agents callable through an API in the OpenAI format.

## What is not promised.

#### An integration promised in advance.

Software is connected only after qualification. Some connections require prior registration with the vendor.

#### Products not yet delivered.

Telephony, camera vision and specialized models are not delivered products; we can study their feasibility, nothing more at this stage. Nexora Vision is a pilot project by invitation only.

→ [See Nexora Vision](/en/vision/)

#### A model that is too small.

A small local model is not always enough for agents that call tools. The model is chosen with the case.

#### A timeline or gain announced.

No universal timeline and no quantified gain: everything is measured on a first case.

## Custom work: your questions.

#### What is the difference from a standard solution?

A standard solution answers a common problem and is configured. Custom work handles a process specific to your company, with its rules and its software.

#### Does the result belong to me?

Your documents, configurations and workflows stay exportable, and the contract provides for it.

#### Is my data used to train a model?

No, never. Your documents, prompts and results are not used to train or fine-tune a model, and are never sold.

#### How much does it cost?

There is no public price yet for custom automation. Two things are paid for. Support (assessment, installation, configuration): a written estimate follows the assessment and says what is included and what is not. Use of the platform: in credits, because anything that calls an AI model uses them; a plan gives you credits and storage every month, otherwise you top up. Platform prices will appear on the Pricing page once they are published.

#### What uses credits in an automation?

Each time an agent or a workflow calls an AI model hosted by Nexora AI to read, sort or write, it uses credits at the model catalogue price. Steps that call no model use none. When none are left, the call is refused with a message pointing to top-ups and plans. The credit platform is not yet open for sale.

#### Who maintains the system after go-live?

Nexora AI runs it with you and evolves it. The exact split is written in the contract.
