## Three processes, three builds.

Custom work begins where standard solutions stop. Three constructed examples that do not look alike; they are not customer results.

- **A contractor finds past quotes.** They want to start from comparable earlier quotes to prepare a new one, and track real costs afterward. We would connect the quote base, an estimating agent and an approval before anything is sent. For construction, OPSYS-AI already covers part of this path, with its statuses.
- **A distributor processes supplier documents.** Delivery slips, order confirmations and invoices arrive in different formats. The workflow reads them, compares them with each other and flags what does not match, before any action.
- **A firm prepares case files.** It gathers authorized documents, draws a summary and a list of points to verify, then hands over to the responsible person. Access rights are defined before the build.

## How we work.

#### 1. Observe.

We look at how the work is done: where time is lost, what information comes in, who decides.

#### 2. Prototype.

We describe the workflow in plain language; WorkStudio's Copilot proposes a first version that we review and correct.

#### 3. Connect.

We connect your software through connectors, after checking that each one can be.

#### 4. Test.

We build a test set from your real cases and measure whether the agent answers well, before changing anything.

#### 5. Approve.

Every step that commits the business waits for a person. We decide with you which ones.

#### 6. Train and operate.

Your team learns to supervise and correct; we track what works and adjust the rest.

## What we build with.

Everything is built in [Nexora WorkStudio](/en/workstudio/), which keeps the system readable and portable. Its components are delivered; it is their assembly for your case that is custom.

- **Agents.** Assistants that follow your instructions, use your tools and documents, and cite their sources.
- **Workflows.** Sequences of steps triggered by an email, a schedule, a request, with human approvals.
- **Documents and data.** Knowledge bases, reading of scanned documents, tables, batch processing.
- **Connectors and an API.** Services linked by Nexora Connect, and agents callable by API in the OpenAI format.

## What is not promised.

#### An integration promised in advance.

Software is connected only after qualification. Some connections require prior registration with the provider.

#### Products not delivered.

Telephony, camera vision and specialized models are not delivered products; we can study their feasibility, nothing more at this stage.

#### A model that is too small.

A small local model is not always enough for agents that call tools. The model is chosen with the case.

#### An announced timeline or gain.

No universal timeline and no quantified gain: everything is measured on a first case.

## Custom work: your questions.

#### How is it different from a standard solution?

A standard solution addresses a common problem and is configured. Custom work handles a process unique to your business, with its rules and its software.

#### Does the result belong to me?

Your documents, configurations and workflows stay exportable, and the contract provides for it.

#### How much does it cost?

There is no public price yet. After the diagnosis, we provide a written estimate that states what is included and what is not.

#### Who maintains the system after go-live?

We run it with you and keep it evolving. The exact split is written in the contract.
