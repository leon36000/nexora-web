## Five commitments on your data.

Each one can be checked before and after go-live. Outbound flows (updates, licences, a possible external model) are listed before go-live: installing on your own server does not, by itself, mean there is no outbound traffic.

#### On premises, everything stays with you.

Your data stays on your server. Nexora AI only reaches it if you authorize it in writing, for a specific, time-limited task, and every access is logged.

#### Hosted, everything stays in Quebec.

Your data is stored and processed in our infrastructure, in Quebec, backups included. The only exceptions are services you explicitly turn on, all listed in a subcontractor register.

#### Never used for training.

Nexora AI never uses your data, documents, prompts or results to train or fine-tune a model, whether it belongs to Nexora AI or to a third party.

#### Never sold.

Nexora AI does not sell, rent, trade or share for advertising any personal information or business data entrusted to it.

#### No external model without activation.

Your data only goes to an external model provider if you turned it on for a specific agent or project. The list of providers and the contracts that set out retention and training are in preparation.

## What makes them checkable.

A commitment is worth its proof. Here are the pieces that back it.

#### A processing contract.

Signed with each customer. The standard contract and data processing agreement are in preparation.

#### A subcontractor register.

Each one's country and role. The register is in preparation.

#### An access log.

Who reaches what, and when. On premises, every access by Nexora AI to your system is logged.

#### A data-flow diagram.

Where data is stored, which external services are called, which backups exist: handed over in writing, before go-live.

#### No silent switch.

No call to an external AI service is configured without your explicit activation, recorded in writing, and the system never switches silently to one.

#### A way out.

Your documents, configurations and workflows stay exportable; the contract provides for it.

#### A person decides.

In the workflows we set up, any action that commits the business waits for a person's approval, unless you decide otherwise in writing.

#### No claim without proof.

We publish no testimonial, no figure and no certification that we cannot prove. When a capability is in pilot or planned, we write it.

WorkStudio functions, verified on Nexora AI's test server. They support your process; they do not replace it.

Allow, mask or refuse sending personal information to a model outside the instance, by workspace.

Each model shows where it runs; connector credentials are encrypted and kept on your instance.

## Three planes of data.

When we say “data”, we mean three different things. We treat them separately. [Read our commitments on your data](/en/your-data/).

Missing a document? [Ask for a document](/en/contact/).

- **The site's public content.** What you read here: made to be published and indexed.
- **Your exchanges with Nexora AI.** Forms, emails, billing: they serve only to answer you and to serve you. The site's privacy policy, in preparation, will describe them.
- **Your system's data.** Your documents, conversations and databases: they stay inside the agreed perimeter, described by the data-flow diagram.

## Your premises, a separate project.

The physical security of your premises is outside this trust center. [Nexora Vision](/en/vision/), our AI video surveillance project, is a pilot project by invitation: it is not an available product, and no installation exists yet.

- **No facial recognition.** Nexora Vision does not offer facial recognition: the function is not delivered.
- **No employee analysis.** No function analyzing productivity or behaviour; alerts are limited to the zones and hours you define.
- **A person verifies.** An alert asks a person to check: the AI detects people and objects, not identity or intent.
