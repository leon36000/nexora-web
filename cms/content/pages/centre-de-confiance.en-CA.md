## Verifiable commitments.

Each one can be checked before and after go-live.

#### You know where your data goes.

Before any go-live, we hand over in writing the data-flow diagram: where data is stored, which external services are called, which backups exist.

#### No external model without consent.

No call to an external AI service is configured without your written agreement, and the system never switches to one without telling you.

#### Your data stays yours.

We do not reuse one customer's data for another customer or to train a model without that customer's specific authorization.

#### You can leave.

Your documents, configurations and workflows stay exportable; the contract provides for it.

#### Decisions stay human.

In the workflows we set up, any action that commits the business waits for a person's approval, unless you decide otherwise in writing.

#### No claim without proof.

We publish no testimonial, no figure and no certification that we cannot prove. When a capability is in pilot or planned, we write it.

## Three planes of data.

When we say “data”, we mean three different things. We treat them separately.

- **The site's public content.** What you read here: made to be published and indexed.
- **Your exchanges with Nexora.** Forms, emails, billing: they serve only to answer you and to serve you. The site's privacy policy, in preparation, will describe them.
- **Your system's data.** Your documents, conversations and databases: they stay inside the agreed perimeter, described by the data-flow diagram.

## What we do not claim.

#### No customer named.

We publish no customer name, no logo, no case study until we have the written agreement of the company concerned.

#### No figure without measurement.

Neither time saved, nor success rate, nor savings: they are measured at your site, during a pilot.

#### No announced service level.

Availability and response times are set out in the contract.

#### No decreed compliance.

A company's compliance depends on its practices, its contracts and its governance. No supplier confers it.

## Trust: your questions.

#### How do I ask for a document?

Write to us through the contact form and name the document. While it is in preparation, we say so instead of promising you a date.

#### Do you have a security attestation?

We claim none today. We describe what the software allows and what we document.

#### Who at Nexora can access my system?

Only the access agreed in advance, described in the deployment study. In the on-premises mode, you grant it and can withdraw it.

#### Do you use an external model?

Only if you accepted it in writing. It then appears on the data-flow diagram as an outbound flow.
