## Five commitments on your data.

You can check each one before and after go-live. Installing on your own server does not mean there is no outbound traffic: we list updates, licences and any external model before go-live.

#### On premises, everything stays with you.

Your data stays on your server. Nexora AI only reaches it if you authorize it in writing, for a specific, time-limited task, and every access is logged.

#### Hosted, everything stays in Quebec.

Your data is stored and processed in our infrastructure, in Quebec, backups included. The only exceptions are services you explicitly turn on, all listed in a subcontractor register.

#### Never used for training.

Nexora AI never uses your data, documents, prompts or results to train or fine-tune a model, whether it belongs to Nexora AI or to a third party.

#### Never sold.

Nexora AI does not sell, rent, trade or share for advertising any personal information or business data entrusted to it.

#### No external model without activation.

Your data only goes to an external model provider if you turned it on for a specific agent or project. The list of providers and the contracts that set out retention and training are in preparation.

## Three planes of data.

When we say “data”, we mean three different things. We treat them separately. [Read our commitments on your data](/en/your-data/).

Missing a document? [Ask for a document](/en/contact/).

To judge a provider, see the [IT director's checklist](/en/guides/sovereign-ai-checklist/).

- **The site's public content.** What you read here: made to be published and indexed.
- **Your exchanges with Nexora AI.** Forms, emails, billing: they serve only to answer you and to serve you. The site's privacy policy, in preparation, will describe them.
- **Your system's data.** Your documents, conversations and databases: they stay inside the agreed perimeter, described by the data-flow diagram. On the credit platform, your files count toward your plan's storage, and the model gateway keeps only usage metadata.

## Trust: your questions.

#### How do I ask for a document?

Write to us through the contact form and name the document. While it is in preparation, we say so instead of promising you a date.

#### Is my data used to train an AI?

No, never. Nexora AI uses neither your data, nor your documents, nor your prompts, nor your results to train or fine-tune a model, whether it belongs to Nexora AI or to a third party.

#### Is my data sold?

No. Nexora AI does not sell, rent, trade or share for advertising any personal information or business data entrusted to it.

#### Do you have a security attestation?

We claim no security attestation and no certification today. We describe what the software allows and what we document.

#### Who at Nexora AI can access my system?

Only the access agreed in advance, described in the deployment study. On premises, Nexora AI only reaches it if you authorize it in writing, for a specific, time-limited task, and every access is logged.

#### Do you use an external model?

Yes, but only if you turned it on in writing, for a specific agent or project. The external model then appears on the data-flow diagram as an outbound flow.

#### Where is my data on the credit platform?

The documents and files you keep with Nexora AI use your plan's storage, counted per organization across all products. The model gateway keeps only usage metadata (model, tokens, cost, duration), never the content of requests or answers. Each model shows its hosting location and its provider's data policy, and Nexora Privacy Router evaluates every request before the call. That location is information, not a guarantee of data residency.
