#### What to adapt here.

Translation into English or French follows the model's usage, not your industry's: have a binding text reviewed by a person who reads the target language.

#### What applies to all.

The templates are written for a Quebec small business: amounts in Canadian dollars, GST and QST wherever a tax applies, and Quebec business vocabulary. They do not guarantee any tax or legal compliance: the examples are fictional, and your accountant or adviser validates the result before use.

## Your questions about this template.

#### Does this template send anything for me?

No. It produces text to review; you use or send it yourself. Only five templates in the collection send an email, and each waits first for a person's approval.

#### Does the translation follow Quebec French usage?

It asks for it: the instruction requests Quebec vocabulary for French and Canadian spelling for English. That is a request made to a model, not a guarantee: have a binding text reviewed.

#### Is it suited to a Quebec business?

It is written for Quebec but still needs validating: the examples are fictional, and the template does not replace your accountant or adviser. Review every result before using it and adapt the instructions to your business.
