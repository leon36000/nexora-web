Nexora RAG turns your documents into a knowledge base that AI queries, showing its sources. You create it in one click from your panel, then connect it to Nexora Code, to a bot or to another tool with an API key.

You will pay for the storage it uses, counted in your plan's, and for the tokens that index your documents and rank the answers (embedding and rerank), in credits. None of this is delivered, no price is published and no date is announced. [See the Nexora RAG page](/en/apps/rag/).

## Corpus: two distinct destinations.

Nexora Corpus is meant to turn assorted files into cleaner, structured, traceable, usable data. What we describe here is an intention, not a delivered product.

It will distinguish two destinations that must not be confused: updating a document base and training a model are two different operations, with distinct costs, risks and approvals.

- **Knowledge for retrieval.** Extraction, classification, deduplication, access rights, provenance, versions, indexing: what it takes to find an answer in your documents, with its sources.
- **Data to specialize your own model.** Selection, quality, authorizations, usage rights, separation between training and evaluation, regression control.
- **A starting rule.** An imported document does not authorize its use for training. No data goes automatically to both destinations, and Nexora AI never uses your data to train or fine-tune a model, except your own, at your written request.

## Suite: your questions.

#### Can I buy Nexora Code or Nexora RAG?

Not yet. Both are in preparation, with no date, and sales are not open. Each page says what is ready and what is coming, and its waiting list lets you know at launch.

#### Can I buy Nexora Corpus?

Not today: no availability or date is announced. As soon as a product exists, this page will say so.

#### How do credits work across the suite?

A plan gives you credits every month and storage space; without a plan, you top up credits. Anything that calls an AI model uses them, in every product of the suite. Prices are not published: they will appear on the Pricing page.

#### Are Quanta or Helix better than other models?

We publish no comparison. These models do not yet have a canonical sheet with measured results.

#### Will Corpus train a model on my documents?

No, never. Importing a document into a knowledge base does not authorize its use for training, and Nexora AI never uses your data to train or fine-tune a model, except your own, at your written request.

#### What can I use right now?

Nexora WorkStudio, as a demonstration on request. For construction and field services, OPSYS-AI, a product in development, with the availability level of each of its departments.
