## VPS in Quebec: your questions.

#### Can I order a VPS today?

No. The offers are in preparation: no plan is published, no price is shown and payments work in test mode only. Write to us to discuss it.

#### Which AI applications will be installed?

Hermes Agent, hardened OpenClaw, Ollama with AnythingLLM, AnythingLLM and LibreChat are planned. For n8n, Open WebUI and Dify, only a guide is provided, because their licence requires it.

#### Where is the server hosted?

Nexora AI is preparing servers hosted in Quebec. The technical evidence of the location (address, contract, backups, subprocessors) is in preparation and will be provided in writing.

#### How much will a VPS cost?

No price is published. When plans are, the VPS page will show them in Canadian dollars, before tax.

#### How do you access the server?

By an SSH key: you paste your public key when ordering. Nexora AI never asks for your private key.
