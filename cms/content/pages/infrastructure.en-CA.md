#### Which format should I choose?

It depends on your models, how many people use them and where your data must stay. The consultation determines it; you receive a written recommendation.

Often, yes, if it suits the target models. We check before any proposal.

#### Who handles maintenance?

We do, if you wish: updates, monitoring and support are part of the operations service.

#### How much does it cost?

There is no public price yet. Two things are paid for: our services (a written estimate follows the consultation) and platform usage, in credits. Prices will appear on the Pricing page as soon as they are published.
