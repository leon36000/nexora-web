## Your cameras, finally watched.

Security systems record everything, but the video is only reviewed afterwards, often too late. Our AIs connect to your existing cameras and security system and analyze the images continuously.

When suspicious behaviour appears (presence in a closed area, intrusion after business hours, someone loitering), the alert goes out while the event is happening.

## Detect, alert, document.

#### Connected to what you have.

Your current cameras and security system are usually suitable; compatibility is checked during the consultation.

#### Real-time detection.

Presence in a restricted area, movement after closing, a vehicle that lingers: the AI spots what you ask it to watch for.

#### Immediate alerts.

By email, by text, on the dashboard or in the mobile app, depending on urgency and time of day.

#### Following your instructions.

It can notify your security officers, your monitoring centre or the police, according to the rules you set in writing.

#### A report for every event.

Every suspicious activity produces a report: the timeline, the video clips and the alerts sent.

#### Find the right clip.

Across weeks of recordings, the timeline takes you straight to the moment that matters.

## Your questions about surveillance.

#### Do I need to replace my cameras?

Not necessarily. Most recent network cameras can be analyzed; the consultation checks this camera by camera.

#### Can the AI make mistakes?

Yes. It can flag a harmless event or miss one. The rules are tuned with you to reduce false alarms, and every alert comes with the video clip so you can judge quickly.

#### Who receives the alerts?

The people and services you designate: you, your officers, your monitoring centre or the police, depending on severity and time of day.

#### How much does it cost?

There is no public price yet. Two things are paid for: our services (a written estimate follows the consultation) and platform usage, in credits. Prices will appear on the Pricing page as soon as they are published.
