## Sovereign AI, in five commitments.

Five commitments in plain language, backed by a data processing agreement signed with each client. The standard agreement and the subcontractor register are in preparation.

#### On premises, everything stays with you.

Your data stays on your server. Nexora AI only reaches it if you authorize it in writing, for a specific, time-limited task, and every access is logged.

#### Hosted, everything stays in Quebec.

Your data is stored and processed in our infrastructure, in Quebec, backups included. The only exceptions are services you explicitly turn on, all listed in a subcontractor register.

#### Never used for training.

Nexora AI never uses your data, documents, prompts or results to train or fine-tune a model, whether it belongs to Nexora AI or to a third party.

#### Never sold.

Nexora AI does not sell, rent, trade or share for advertising any personal information or business data entrusted to it.

#### No external model without activation.

Your data only goes to an external model provider if you turned it on for a specific agent or project. The list of providers and the contracts that set out retention and training are in preparation.

## Sovereignty you can check.

“Sovereign” here means control, not a label. AI is sovereign when four questions have a precise written answer that your team can check.

Sovereignty does not shield you from the law: it reduces the dependencies you did not choose. The laws that apply to your business, and your obligations, stay the same.

- **Where it runs.** On a server you own, or in Nexora AI's infrastructure in Quebec. The location is written down, not assumed.
- **Who can reach it.** Named accounts, roles and a log of actions. Nexora AI's own access to your system is agreed in advance.
- **Where the data goes.** A data-flow diagram, handed over in writing before go-live, shows what stays inside the perimeter and what leaves it.
- **Whether you can leave.** Your documents, configurations and workflows stay exportable, and the contract says so.

## Four layers of control.

A server in Quebec says nothing about the models it calls. So we look at each layer separately and write down what we document.

#### 1. The data.

Your documents, conversations, images and databases. We document where they are stored, who can reach them, how long they are kept and how they are exported or erased when the contract ends.

→ [See the five commitments](/en/your-data/)

#### 2. The models.

The engine that produces the answers can run on your server, in our infrastructure or with a third party. In WorkStudio and in the model catalogue, each model shows where it is hosted and what its provider does with data. No external model is called without your explicit activation, recorded in writing, and the system never switches silently to another model when one fails.

→ [See WorkStudio](/en/workstudio/)

#### 3. The compute.

The machines that run the models: your own server, or our infrastructure in Quebec. The hardware, the hosting location and the subcontractors are named in the deployment study.

→ [Compare the modes](/en/deployment/)

#### 4. The operations.

Who installs, updates, monitors and repairs. Updates, licences and any outbound traffic are listed: installing on your own server does not, by itself, mean there is no outbound traffic.

## Three questions for any provider.

We name no one and judge no one. These are general observations about industry practice, and three questions to ask any AI provider in writing, ourselves included.

Ask us these three questions: [review my project](/en/contact/?sujet=etude-deploiement).

To go further, read [Where your data really is](/en/guides/where-is-your-ai-data/) and the [IT director's checklist](/en/guides/sovereign-ai-checklist/).

- **Do my conversations improve the model?** In some consumer AI services, sharing conversations to improve the models is a setting the user has to switch off personally. At Nexora AI, the answer is written down: never.
- **Stored in Canada, processed where?** Some AI providers advertise data storage in Canada while requests are processed elsewhere: the difference between storage and processing is a question to ask. In our hosted mode, data is stored and processed in Quebec, backups included.
- **Which country can demand access?** A provider subject to another country's jurisdiction may be required to hand over data it holds, even data stored in Canada (for example, a U.S. law passed in 2018). Canadian and Quebec laws also apply to businesses here. “Sovereign” describes control, not immunity.

## Law 25 and your business.

Law 25 (Quebec's private-sector privacy law) is addressed to your business, not to your supplier. No software and no supplier makes a business compliant: responsibility stays with the business that holds the information.

Our part: give you what you need to answer, in writing, the questions the law requires you to ask. [See the five commitments on your data](/en/your-data/).

- **What the law asks of your business.** A designated person in charge, published governance rules, a privacy impact assessment for any project involving personal information and, before communicating information outside Quebec, an assessment and a written agreement.
- **What Nexora AI provides.** A data-flow diagram that says where the data goes, an access log, a subcontractor register and template documents, including a privacy impact assessment template. These documents are in preparation.
- **What stays with you.** Informing the people concerned, justifying a decision based on automated processing and answering access requests. No AI takes these over for you.

## What Nexora AI does not promise.

Here is what we do not promise, and why.

#### Zero risk.

No system is exempt from it. We reduce exposure and we describe it; we do not declare it nonexistent.

#### Legal immunity.

A server in Quebec, or on your premises, does not by itself set aside the laws that apply to your business and your suppliers.

#### Automatic compliance.

No software and no supplier makes a business compliant with privacy law: that depends on its practices, its contracts and its governance. We are preparing template documents to help; they are not available yet.

#### No outbound traffic at all.

Updates, licences, possible external models: every outbound flow is listed, and we only say “no data leaves” once we have checked it on your configuration.

#### “Total” sovereignty.

The word has no legal definition, and some dependencies (base models, components, hardware) always come from elsewhere. We prefer to name each dependency.

## Sovereignty: your questions.

#### Does sovereign mean offline?

Not automatically. A system installed on your server can still receive updates, check a licence or call an external model if you chose that. We list every outbound flow before go-live; running with no outbound flow at all is decided and verified case by case.

#### Is a server in Quebec enough?

No. A server in Quebec is not enough to make AI sovereign: where the machines sit says nothing about the models called, the backups, the logs or the subcontractors. That is why we look at four layers, not one.

#### Is my data used to train a model?

No, never. Nexora AI uses neither your data, nor your documents, nor your prompts, nor your results to train or fine-tune a model, whether it belongs to Nexora AI or to a third party.

#### Is my data sold?

No. Nexora AI does not sell, rent, trade or share for advertising any personal information or business data entrusted to it.

#### Can you prove where my data is?

Yes, in writing. We hand over the data flow diagram of your installation and, in hosted mode, the location of hosting, backups and logs and the list of subcontractors. You can have anyone you choose check these documents.

#### What if I want to use an external model?

It is possible, on two conditions: you activate it in writing, and it appears on the data flow diagram as an outbound flow. In WorkStudio, each model shows whether it runs on your server, in Europe or with a third party.

#### What happens to my data when the contract ends?

Your documents, configurations and workflows stay exportable, and the contract provides for it. The deployment study describes the retention and deletion that apply to your case.

#### On the credit platform, what does the gateway see?

The model gateway keeps only usage metadata (model, tokens, cost, duration), never the content of your requests or of the answers. Each model in the catalogue shows its hosting location and its provider's data policy, and Nexora Privacy Router evaluates every request before the call. The location shown is information, not a guarantee of data residency.
