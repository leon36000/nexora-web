## Powerful underneath, simple on top.

These solutions are assembled in [Nexora WorkStudio](/en/workstudio/), a single tool that brings what AI can do today within your reach: agents (software that completes a task using the tools you authorize), answers drawn from your own documents, reading of scanned documents, transcription and read-aloud, and side-by-side comparison of several models. "Leading edge" is our way of putting it; the proof is that list.

And it is remarkably simple to use: you describe the need in plain language, a building assistant (the Copilot) prepares a workflow (an automated sequence of tasks) for you to review, then you build it with cards or on a canvas, the way you sketch a diagram, without writing code. The workflow pictured here receives an invoice by email, prepares the entry and stops for a person's approval before writing anything.

Every go-live starts with a diagnosis of your case: we check that your software can be connected, and we announce no universal timeline. These are not off-the-shelf boxes but tailored configurations.

## Powerful, but never alone at the helm.

Whatever the solution, four rules do not change. The data you entrust to us stays with your business, on your server or with us in Quebec, is never used to train a model and is never sold. [Read our commitments on your data](/en/your-data/).

- **A person decides.** In the workflows we set up, any action that commits the business (sending, paying, posting an entry) waits for a person's approval, unless you decide otherwise in writing.
- **Answers cite their sources.** When an agent searches your documents, it numbers and cites what it used.
- **Everything leaves a trace.** Every model call can be consulted: what was asked, what was answered, with which model.
- **You choose where it runs.** On your server, or in our infrastructure in Quebec. An external model comes in only when you explicitly activate it, in writing.

## Before you choose: your questions.

#### Where do we start?

With the problem that costs you the most time. We describe it with you, check which software must be connected, then put a first case into service before expanding.

#### Do I have to change my software?

Generally not: the system connects to what you already use. Whether a given piece of software can be connected depends on the service concerned; we check it during the diagnosis.

#### Is it ready to use?

No, and deliberately so: each solution is configured for your business, with your rules and your documents. What is delivered are the building blocks of WorkStudio.

#### Is my data used to train a model?

Never. It stays on your server or with us in Quebec, is not used to train or fine-tune a model, and is never sold. The Your data page details each commitment.

#### What about the phone?

No call-answering service is delivered today. A voice message dropped in as a recording can be transcribed; live telephony is checked separately, case by case.

#### How much does it cost?

There is no public price yet. After the diagnosis, we provide a written estimate that states what is included and what is not.
