## Powerful, but never alone at the helm.

Whatever the solution, four rules do not change. The data you entrust to us stays with your business, on your server or with us in Quebec, is never used to train a model and is never sold. [Read our commitments on your data](/en/your-data/).

- **A person decides.** In the workflows we set up, any action that commits the business (sending, paying, posting an entry) waits for a person's approval, unless you decide otherwise in writing.
- **Answers cite their sources.** When an agent searches your documents, it numbers and cites what it used.
- **Everything leaves a trace.** Every model call can be consulted: what was asked, what was answered, with which model.
- **You choose where it runs.** On your server, or in our infrastructure in Quebec. An external model comes in only when you explicitly activate it, in writing.

## Before you choose: your questions.

#### Where do I start with AI in my small business?

With the problem that costs you the most time. We describe it with you, check which software must be connected, then put a first case into service before expanding. A diagnosis opens the process.

#### Do I have to change my software?

Generally not: the system connects to what you already use. Whether a given piece of software can be connected depends on the service concerned; we check it during the diagnosis.

#### Are the solutions ready to use?

No, and deliberately so: each solution is configured for your business, with your rules and your documents. What is delivered are the building blocks of WorkStudio; assembling them for your case is done with you.

#### Is my data used to train a model?

Never. Your data stays on your server or with us in Quebec, is not used to train or fine-tune a model, and is never sold. The Your data page details each commitment.

#### Do the solutions answer the phone?

No. No call-answering service is delivered today. A voice message dropped in as a recording can be transcribed; live telephony is checked separately, case by case.

#### How much does it cost?

There is no public price yet. Two things are paid for. Support (assessment, installation, configuration): a written estimate follows the assessment and says what is included and what is not. Use of the platform: in credits, because anything that calls an AI model uses them; a plan gives you credits and storage every month, otherwise you top up. Platform prices will appear on the Pricing page once they are published.

#### What uses credits?

Any call to an AI model: reading a document, drafting a reply, sorting a message. One credit equals one Canadian cent of tokens at the model catalogue price. Tools that call no model use none. When none are left, the call is refused with a message pointing to top-ups and plans.

#### Can I open an account and buy credits now?

Not yet. The credit platform is not open for sale: accounts and payments only work in test mode and no price is published. You can book a diagnosis now, though; prices will appear on the Pricing page once they are published.
