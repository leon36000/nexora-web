## To adapt before use.

#### What to adapt here.

The GST and QST registration numbers and the Quebec enterprise number (NEQ) stay in square brackets, to fill in. A service exempt from tax, or a business that is not registered, is flagged in brackets rather than guessed. Only the federal GST and the QST are covered: other provinces' taxes, such as the harmonized sales tax, are not.

#### What applies to all.

The templates are written for a Quebec small business: amounts in Canadian dollars, GST and QST wherever a tax applies, and Quebec business vocabulary. They do not guarantee any tax or legal compliance: the examples are fictional, and your accountant or adviser validates the result before use.

#### Nothing is sent.

The template produces text to review. Only five templates in the collection send an email, and each waits first for a person's approval.

## Your questions about this template.

#### Does this template send anything for me?

No. It produces text to review; you use or send it yourself. Only five templates in the collection send an email, and each waits first for a person's approval.

#### Does the template compute GST and QST?

Yes, through its instructions: GST at 5% and QST at 9.975%, each on the subtotal before tax and rounded to the cent. An agent does the calculation from the text; it is not accounting software, so review the amounts before sending the quote.

#### Does it suit my business?

It starts from a quote by a Quebec business, with fictional examples. Your tax registration status, exempt services and terms are not guessed: your accountant or adviser validates the quote before it goes out.
