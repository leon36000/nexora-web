## Your questions. Our answers.

#### Does my data leave Quebec?

It depends on the deployment mode and the models you choose, and we write it down before the start. At your site, your data stays on your server. At ours, it stays in Quebec, backups included. In both cases, the only exits are those we list with you before go-live: updates, licences and, if you enable one, an external model. We write "no data leaves" only if we have verified it on your configuration.

#### Do you train your models with our data?

Never. We do not use your data, documents, prompts or outputs to train or fine-tune any model, ours or a third party's, and we put it in the contract.

#### Do you sell our data?

Never. We do not sell, rent, exchange or share for advertising purposes any data entrusted to us.

#### Is it really simple to use?

That is the goal of the whole tool. You describe in plain language what you want to automate, an assistant prepares the workflow, you review it, try it step by step, then launch it. We install the system, run it with you and train your team in use and supervision, not in technology.

#### We have no technical staff.

That is not an obstacle. We install the system, run it with you and keep it evolving; your team's training covers use and supervision, not technology.

#### What happens if we stop?

You leave with your documents, configurations and workflows: export is provided for in the contract from the start, not negotiated at the end.

#### How much does it cost?

There is no public price yet. The cost depends on the number of cases handled, the volume of documents, the deployment mode, the integrations and, at your site, the hardware. After a diagnostic, we give you a written estimate stating what is included and what is not. Self-service offers will show their prices and renewal conditions when they open.

#### Will AI replace my employees?

Our systems take over repetitive tasks; the decision stays with a person. A workflow can stop for an approval before acting, and a log keeps track of what was done.

#### Why not a consumer assistant?

A consumer assistant answers general questions well. What Nexora AI adds: the choice of where your requests are processed, your own documents as the basis for answers, links with your software, approvals and a log. The right choice depends on what you want to do with it.

#### Do we need a powerful server?

It depends on the case. At our site, you buy no server. At yours, we size the hardware to your uses: number of users, volume of documents, chosen model. The diagnostic answers this before any purchase.

#### What is an AI agent?

Software that carries out a task for you, with the tools and documents you allow it, and that asks for your approval before an important action.
