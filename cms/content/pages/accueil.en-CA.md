## Your questions. Our answers.

#### Does my data leave Quebec?

It depends on the deployment mode and the models you choose, and we put it in writing before we start. On your premises, the hardware and the access are yours, and the only outbound flows are the ones we list together: updates, licences, and an external model if you choose one. In our infrastructure, hosting is in Quebec, and we specify backups, logs and service providers in writing. In both cases, we only write “no data leaves” if we have verified it on your configuration.

#### Do you train your models on our data?

No, not without your specific authorization. Our rule: a customer's data is not used for another customer or to train a model without that customer's consent, and we write it into the contract.

#### We have no technical staff.

That is not an obstacle. We install the system, run it with you and keep it evolving; training for your team covers use and supervision, not the technical side.

#### What happens if we stop?

You leave with your documents, your configurations and your workflows: export is written into the contract from the start, not negotiated at the end.

#### How much does it cost?

There is no public price yet. The cost depends on the number of use cases, the volume of documents, the deployment mode, the integrations and, on your premises, the hardware. After a diagnostic, we provide a written estimate that states what is included and what is not. Self-service offers will show their prices and renewal terms when they open.

#### Will AI replace my employees?

Our systems take over repetitive tasks; the decision stays with a person. A workflow can stop for an approval before it acts, and a log keeps a record of what was done.

#### Why not a general-purpose assistant such as ChatGPT?

A general-purpose assistant answers general questions well. What Nexora adds: a choice of where your requests are processed, your own documents as the basis for answers, links to your software, approvals and a log. The right choice depends on what you want to do with it.

#### Do we need a powerful server?

It depends on the use case. With us, you buy no server. On your premises, we size the hardware to your usage: number of users, volume of documents, chosen model. The diagnostic answers this question before you buy anything.

#### What is an AI agent?

Software that carries out a task for you, with the tools and documents you authorize, and that asks for your approval before an important action.
