## Month-end means data entry.

A small accounting firm receives invoices, expense reports and statements from its clients, in every possible form. A large share of month-end time goes to rereading amounts and retyping lines, rather than advising.

The example is constructed to illustrate the principle; it is not a customer's result.

- **Documents in every format.** Emails, photos, scans, PDFs.
- **Checks to repeat.** Pre-tax amounts, taxes and total must agree.
- **Advisory time lost.** What the client really pays for is the accountant's judgment.

## What the system does.

The accountant defines the fields and the checking rules; the system applies them document by document.

#### It reads the documents.

Invoices, quotes, purchase orders, expense reports: starter templates exist for these four types.

#### It checks.

It verifies that pre-tax amounts and taxes add up to the total, to within a few cents, and marks doubtful documents.

#### It prepares.

One pre-entry line per document, with the fields agreed with the accountant (category, supplier, project).

#### It waits.

The accountant reviews the lines, corrects and approves. Nothing is recorded before.

## What your team receives.

A table of documents ready for review, with discrepancies first. The expected benefit: less retyping and errors caught sooner. No quantified gain is announced; it is measured during a pilot, on your own files.

For construction companies, [OPSYS-AI](/en/opsys-ai/) goes further: its in-house finance department (M5) prepares a draft invoice after the work and tracks receivables. That department is in pilot, and neither sending invoices nor payroll calculation is delivered.

- **A pre-entry table.** One row per document, with the “to verify” flag.
- **The reasons for discrepancies.** Missing field, unreadable value, total that does not add up.
- **A file reviewed, not rekeyed.** The accountant spends time on judgment.

## Accounting: your questions.

#### Does the AI do the bookkeeping?

No. It prepares the pre-entry and flags discrepancies. Entries and decisions remain your accountant's.

#### Can bank reconciliation be automated?

Not with a product delivered today. A custom study is possible, from exported statements, without a bank connection.

#### Does my accountant have to change tools?

In principle, no: the pre-entry is filed in a table, and what follows depends on the software they use, which we qualify with them.

#### Do my documents leave my server?

Not if reading is done with a model on your server. An external reading service is possible, only with your written agreement, and it is disclosed.
