## Month-end means data entry.

A small accounting firm receives invoices, expense reports and statements from its clients, in every shape imaginable. A large share of month-end time goes to rereading amounts and retyping lines, rather than advising.

The example is constructed to illustrate the principle; it is not a customer's result.

- **Documents in every format.** Email, photos, scans, PDFs.
- **Checks to repeat.** The pre-tax amounts, the taxes and the total must agree.
- **Advice time lost.** What the client really pays for is the accountant's judgment.

## From document to pre-entry.

The accountant defines the fields and the control rules; the system applies them document by document, with WorkStudio's AI document reading.

#### It reads the documents.

Invoices, quotes, purchase orders, expense reports: starter templates exist for these four types.

#### It checks.

It verifies that pre-tax amounts and taxes add up to the total, to within a few cents, and marks doubtful documents.

#### It prepares.

One pre-entry line per document, with the fields agreed with the accountant (category, supplier, project).

#### It waits.

The accountant reviews the lines, corrects and approves. Nothing is recorded before that.

## The accountant has the last word.

#### The accountant keeps the decision.

No entry is recorded without their approval.

#### No bank link.

Bank reconciliation is not delivered in WorkStudio. It can be studied as custom work, from statements you export, with no connection to your bank.

#### Your software, to be qualified.

Writing to your accounting software goes through a connector that is qualified during the diagnosis, software by software.

#### Your tax rules.

The document-reading templates are not yet adapted to Quebec. Quebec taxes, categories and chart of accounts are defined with you and checked on your documents.

#### No accounting advice.

The system prepares and checks; it does not advise. The decisions stay with your accountant.

#### Your data stays yours.

On your server or with us, in Quebec: never used to train a model, never sold. An external reading service comes in only when you explicitly activate it, in writing.

## Accounting: your questions.

#### Does the AI do the bookkeeping?

No. It prepares the pre-entry and flags discrepancies. The entries and the decisions stay with your accountant.

#### Can bank reconciliation be automated?

Not with a product delivered today. A custom study is possible, from exported statements, with no bank connection.

#### Does my accountant have to change tools?

Not in principle: the pre-entry is filed in a table, and what follows depends on the software they use, which we qualify with them.

#### Do my documents leave my server?

Not if reading is done with a model on your server. An external reading service is possible, only with your written agreement, and it is disclosed.
