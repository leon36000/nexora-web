## The short answer.

The Weekly project summary template turns your notes into a summary: progress, risks in a table, decisions to make and priorities. A person approves it, and only their approval emails it, with the project name in the subject.

A workflow template is an automated sequence of tasks, provided as a starting point in WorkStudio and to be adapted to the business before it is deployed.

## The steps.

#### Write the summary.

An agent presents progress, risks (probability, impact, measure), decisions to make (who, by when) and priorities, with no invented figure or date.

#### Get approval.

A person reads the summary and approves or refuses it.

#### Send by email.

If approved, the summary goes to the recipient.

## To adapt before use.

#### What to adapt here.

The probability and impact of risks are the agent's estimate from your notes: read them before approving. Set up the mail server.

#### What applies to all.

The templates are written for a Quebec small business: amounts in Canadian dollars, GST and QST wherever a tax applies, and Quebec business vocabulary. They do not guarantee any tax or legal compliance: the examples are fictional, and your accountant or adviser validates the result before use.

#### A person approves before sending.

The flow stops for a person's approval and sends the email only if they approve. The mail server (host, port, account, sender) is left to fill in.

## Your questions about this template.

#### Can it send an email without my approval?

No. The flow stops for a person's approval and sends the email only if they approve. The mail server must be filled in.

#### Does the summary go out by itself on a schedule?

No. The template runs when you start it and stops for a person's approval. A scheduled run is a trigger matter, to set up with you.

#### Is it suited to a Quebec business?

It is written for Quebec but still needs validating: the examples are fictional, and the template does not replace your accountant or adviser. Review every result before using it and adapt the instructions to your business.
