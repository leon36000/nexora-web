#### What to adapt here.

At level three, the instruction cites no rate and no indemnity: late fees or interest appear only if they are already on the invoice or in your terms. The note "to be validated by your adviser" is a reminder that a formal notice commits you: have it reviewed before sending.

The templates are written for a Quebec small business: amounts in Canadian dollars, GST and QST wherever a tax applies, and Quebec business vocabulary. They do not guarantee any tax or legal compliance: the examples are fictional, and your accountant or adviser validates the result before use.

## Your questions about this template.

#### Does this template send anything for me?

No. It produces text to review; you use or send it yourself. Only five templates in the collection send an email, and each waits first for a person's approval.

#### Does the formal notice email have legal value?

The template drafts text, not a legal notice. Have a formal notice reviewed by the competent person before sending it, taking Quebec law into account.

#### Is it suited to a Quebec invoice?

It is written for a Quebec invoice, with the amount taxes included in Canadian dollars, but it knows neither your contract nor your payment terms. A formal notice is to be validated by your adviser before sending.
