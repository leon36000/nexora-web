## From first meeting to operation.

#### 1. Understand.

We observe how the work is done today: where time is lost, what information comes in, who decides.
You receive: a written description of the case, its data and its constraints.

#### 2. Integrate.

We connect the software you already use, so information flows without being retyped.
You receive: the list of connections retained, after each one is qualified.

#### 3. Verify.

We test on your real cases, with your approvals, before any go-live.
You receive: the data-flow diagram, and tests rerun at every change.

#### 4. Train.

Your team learns to supervise, correct and decide, not to program.
You receive: a team that knows how to review, approve and refuse.

#### 5. Operate and improve.

We run the system with you, track what works and adjust the rest.
You receive: traces, measured consumption, adjustments decided together.

## Why just one case first.

A well-chosen case goes into service, gets verified and is corrected quickly. It also reveals what your company really expects from the system, which no diagram tells you. We then expand, without multiplying disconnected tools.

- **No universal timeline.** It depends on your situation: software to connect, quality of documents, rules to write. It is measured on a first case.
- **A measured goal, not a promise.** We set with you what we want to observe during the pilot. We announce no percentage and no time saved before measuring it at your site.
- **A written estimate.** After the diagnosis, it states what is included and what is not. There is no public price yet.

## Four checkpoints.

#### Before starting.

The data-flow diagram: where data is stored, which external services are called, which backups exist.

#### Before go-live.

Real cases are tested with your approvals. Every action that commits the business waits for a person, unless you decide otherwise in writing.

#### During operation.

Every model call leaves a trace; consumption is measured; you see what the system does.

#### When the contract ends.

Your documents, configurations and workflows stay exportable, and the contract provides for it.

## Measuring without inventing figures.

We use the tools in [WorkStudio](/en/workstudio/) to measure, on your own cases, what works. The results belong to you and serve to decide: they are never presented here as customer results.

- **Test sets.** Your real questions and the expected answers, replayed at every change of instruction or model.
- **Traces.** What was asked, what was answered, with which model, in how much time.
- **Measured consumption.** Tokens, runs and footprint, by model, workspace and person, with monthly caps.
- **A model test bench.** The Playground compares several models side by side before choosing one.

## Method: your questions.

#### How long does it take to get started?

We announce no universal timeline: it depends on your software, your documents and your rules. It is measured on a first case.

#### What if the first case does not work?

We see it during the pilot, before investing more. We tell you what is blocking, and whether another case, another approach or nothing at all fits better.

#### Will my team have to learn to program?

No. Training covers usage and supervision: reviewing, correcting, approving, refusing.

#### Can we go faster by handling several cases?

It is possible, but we advise against it at the start: one verified case teaches more than a broad, poorly bounded project.
